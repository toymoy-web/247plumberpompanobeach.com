Grease Trap Cleaning in Pompano Beach, FL

Scheduled cleaning that keeps a commercial kitchen compliant and avoids the backup that happens when a trap gets ignored too long.

Why grease trap cleaning is a compliance issue, not just a maintenance one

Broward County and most municipalities require commercial kitchens to maintain grease traps and keep them from discharging grease into the sewer system, with inspections and potential fines for non-compliant properties. Beyond the regulatory side, an overfull trap is also the most common cause of a sudden kitchen drain backup during service, which costs far more in lost business than a scheduled cleaning would have.

How often a trap actually needs cleaning

The real answer depends on kitchen volume and menu, not a fixed calendar interval. A high-volume fryer-heavy kitchen fills a trap faster than a lower-volume café, and the common industry guideline of cleaning once the trap is 25% full by volume is a better standard than a flat monthly or quarterly schedule applied to every property regardless of actual use.

What happens during a cleaning visit

The trap gets pumped out completely, the walls and baffles scraped of accumulated grease, and the interior inspected for damage or wear before being closed back up. Waste grease is hauled away for proper disposal, since it can't legally go down a drain or into regular trash, and we provide documentation of that disposal for any property that needs it for compliance records.

A trap in significantly worse condition than expected, cracked, corroded, or undersized for the kitchen's actual output, gets flagged during the visit rather than just cleaned and left as-is, since a failing trap is a bigger problem than the grease inside it.

Signs a trap needs cleaning sooner than scheduled

A slow kitchen drain, a sewage or rotten smell near the trap, or grease visibly backing up into a floor drain are all signs a scheduled cleaning shouldn't wait for its calendar date. Any commercial kitchen noticing these between scheduled visits should call rather than wait, since an overflowing trap during service hours is a much bigger disruption than an early cleaning.

Working with multiple locations under one account

A restaurant group or franchise operator with several locations across this coverage area can consolidate grease trap scheduling under one account, staggering visits so they don't all land the same week and keeping consistent documentation across every location for whichever authority requests it. This is set up during the first visit rather than requiring a separate conversation per property.

FAQ

Grease Trap Cleaning Questions

How often does our grease trap actually need cleaning?

It depends on kitchen volume, but a common standard is cleaning once the trap reaches about 25% full by volume, which we can help establish a schedule around after the first service.

Is grease trap cleaning legally required?

Yes, most Florida municipalities and Broward County require commercial kitchens to maintain grease traps and can inspect or fine non-compliant properties.

Can this be scheduled outside business hours?

Yes, before opening or after close is standard for this kind of service to avoid disrupting kitchen operations.

What size grease trap does my restaurant need?

Sizing depends on kitchen volume and the number of fixtures draining into it, which is typically determined during initial installation or by local code requirements for the specific type of establishment.

Can grease trap waste be recycled instead of just disposed of?

In some cases, collected grease is sent to facilities that process it for biodiesel or other uses rather than simple disposal, which we can discuss for a property interested in that option.

What happens if a grease trap inspection finds a violation?

We document the issue and can perform the cleaning or repair needed to bring the trap back into compliance, along with records the property can show during a follow-up inspection.

Is there a difference between a grease trap and a grease interceptor?

They serve the same basic function. Interceptor typically refers to a larger, often outdoor unit, while trap usually refers to a smaller indoor unit under a sink, and we service both.

Do you provide emergency grease trap service for an unexpected overflow?

Yes, an overflowing trap during business hours gets treated as an urgent call given how disruptive it is to a working kitchen.

Can grease trap cleaning be bundled with other commercial services?

Yes, many properties combine it with drain maintenance or backflow testing on the same visit.
