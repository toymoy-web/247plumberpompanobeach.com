Emergency Prep

Before We Arrive: If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Lighthouse Point

A burst pipe moves fast in a 1960s canal-front home, especially where cast-iron supply lines have already been corroding for decades. These four steps limit the damage in the minutes before a technician reaches your address.

Shut off the main valve

Find the main water shutoff, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house, and turn it off to stop the flow before it spreads further.

Kill power to wet areas

If water has reached an outlet, switch or appliance, shut off power to that area at the breaker rather than risk touching anything electrical near standing water.

Clear the immediate area

Move furniture, rugs, and anything valuable away from the water, and keep kids and pets clear of the affected space until a technician arrives.
