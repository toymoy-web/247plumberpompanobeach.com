Local Signal

What we see most in Pompano Beach homes

Industry advisory data on Pompano Beach specifically flags aging cast-iron pipe embedded in concrete slabs as a common feature of the city's mid-century housing stock, especially in neighborhoods built before the 1980s. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out over roughly 40 to 50 years, and once that starts, the leak is usually inside the slab rather than somewhere visible.

If a Pompano Beach home was built before 1980 and the water bill has climbed with no visible leak anywhere, that is the exact profile where leak detection makes sense before a hairline crack turns into a slab break. Salt-air exposure this close to the coast speeds the same corrosion along exposed supply lines, valves, and outdoor fixtures, so it is worth checking those too.

That older housing stock is concentrated in neighborhoods like Pompano Beach Highlands, Palm Aire, Cypress Bend, and the streets around Old Pompano near the Hillsboro Inlet Lighthouse, several of which date to the decades before Tradewinds Park and Pompano Beach Airpark anchored the city's more recently developed west side. A technician dispatched to one of these older blocks is generally expecting original slab plumbing before ever seeing the address.

Before You Call

What to check first

A few checks take less than a minute and help the technician show up with the right parts. First, find the main shutoff valve, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house, and confirm it turns freely. If water is actively leaking, shutting this off first limits damage before help arrives.

Next, note whether the water is hot, cold, or both, since that narrows down whether the issue is the water heater, a supply line, or the drain side. If the home was built before 1980, mention that when you call. It changes what a technician checks first, given how common cast-iron pipe is under slabs of that era in Pompano Beach.

Florida's plumbing code is based on the International Plumbing Code, adopted statewide through the Florida Building Code, and licensed contractors work under standards set by the state's Department of Business and Professional Regulation. That code is the baseline every repair on this site is measured against.

My home was built in the 1960s or 70s. Does that change what's likely wrong?

Often yes. Many mid-century Pompano Beach homes have cast-iron pipe under the slab, and that pipe corrodes from the inside over 40 to 50 years. If the home is that age and something feels off with water pressure or the water bill, leak detection is usually the right first call before repiping is on the table.

Are you available overnight and on weekends?

Does being this close to the coast make a difference?

Yes, salt-laden air speeds up corrosion on exposed metal fittings, outdoor hose bibs, and supply lines faster than it would further inland. If fixtures near an exterior wall or outdoor spigot are corroding sooner than expected, that's a normal pattern this close to the water, not a sign of a bad installation.
